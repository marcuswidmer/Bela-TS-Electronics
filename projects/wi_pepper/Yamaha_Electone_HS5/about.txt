Yamaha Electone HS-5 drum samples

23 mono one-shot recordings from a Yamaha Electone HS-5 organ (1987),
originally sampled and distributed as freeware by JMD (jm1200.fr).
Source: /Users/marwidme/Downloads/S760-HS5 DrumKit/HS5DK.OUT
Source page: https://www.jm1200.fr/index.php?r=6

Format: 16-bit PCM WAV, mono, 32000 Hz, original unity note 60.
Audio was extracted directly from the disk image using its stored
one-shot start/end points. No normalization, EQ, de-emphasis filter,
resampling, fades or other audio processing was applied.
Every exported WAV payload was verified byte-for-byte against its
source waveform in the disk image. The source folder was not changed.

This collection is kept separate from the other sample collections.
These are the 23 original waveforms, without the Roland patch
envelopes, filters or additional transposed keyboard mappings baked in.
See Original Readme.txt for the creator's original freeware notice,
and conversion-manifest.json for names, offsets and checksums.
